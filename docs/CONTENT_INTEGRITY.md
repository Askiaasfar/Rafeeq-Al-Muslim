# QURAN AND ISLAMIC CONTENT INTEGRITY STANDARD

## Quran text
- Use a trusted, explicitly identified Quran corpus; record source, edition, script, riwayah where relevant, license and retrieval date.
- Preserve Uthmani orthography, diacritics, verse boundaries, Surah order and page mapping.
- Never generate, rewrite or auto-correct Quran text with an LLM.
- Verify all 114 Surahs, ayah counts and intended 604-page Madani Mushaf mapping.
- Maintain checksums or reproducible validation for canonical text assets.
- Distinguish scripture, translation, Tafsir, transliteration and AI explanation.

## Translation and Tafsir
- Display translator/scholar, work, edition, language, source and license where applicable.
- Do not present translation as Arabic scripture.
- Do not merge editions or silently paraphrase source text.

## Hadith and duas
- Record collection/book, narrator, chapter/number, source edition and grading attribution where available.
- Never invent a hadith, narrator, reference or authenticity grade.
- Distinguish quoted text, translation and explanation.

## Recitation and riwayah
- Store reciter, riwayah, recording source, license and audio version.
- Do not label a recording with unsupported riwayah metadata.
- Verify audio, ayah segmentation, downloads and offline playback.

## Tajweed and AI
- Use reviewed Tajweed learning content.
- AI recitation feedback is assistance, not a fatwa or substitute for a qualified teacher.
- Do not claim phonetic accuracy unless validated against appropriate samples and qualified reviewers.
- Explain uncertainty.

## Prayer times and Qibla
- Show calculation method, timezone and location source; allow manual settings.
- Request location only when needed.
- Handle timezone/date boundaries and device sensor calibration.
- Explain compass limitations; do not hardcode one city universally.

## AI boundaries
- LLMs may explain or navigate approved material but must not create canonical scripture or fabricate citations.
- Ground religious answers in approved sources and show references.
- If evidence is insufficient, say so.

## Release gate
Arabic RTL/diacritics reviewed; canonical text verified; translation/Tafsir metadata and licenses checked; Hadith/dua references validated; audio provenance checked; offline packages tested in airplane mode; no placeholder or AI-generated scripture; corrections versioned and reviewed.
